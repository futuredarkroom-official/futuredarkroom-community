# Future Darkroom Community

[English](README.md) | [简体中文](README.zh-CN.md)

Welcome to the Future Darkroom community! Future Darkroom is a guided-learning technology-sharing site: knowledge graphs lay out learning paths, guiding learners step by step.

- Website: https://futuredarkroom.com
- Here you can: share ideas, ask questions, show your learning results, and help build the community together.

## How to join the discussion

1. Go to the **Discussions** tab above.
2. Click **New discussion**.
3. Pick a category and write your post. That's it!

## Categories

- **Announcements** — Updates from maintainers
- **General** — Chat about anything and everything
- **Ideas** — Share ideas for new features
- **Q&A** — Ask the community for help
- **Show and tell** — Show off something you've made
- **Polls** — Take a vote from the community

Chinese speakers: you can post in any category in Chinese, or start from the Chinese welcome post.

Welcome, every learner and builder!
